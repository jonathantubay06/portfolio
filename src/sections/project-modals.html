A.L.I.S.O.N.

Desktop · Phone · Voice · Multi-model

$0 per tokenfree models, offline fallback
Talk about a build like this →
ProblemI wanted one assistant that runs my PC by voice and follows me to my phone, without paying per token.
ResultA Jarvis-style HUD on my desktop and phone. Her own wake word, voice control of apps and files, reminders, notes and a daily briefing, routed across free cloud models with an offline fallback.

A.L.I.S.O.N. (Adaptive Learning Intelligent System for Operational Neural assistance) is my own desktop assistant. A small router hands the model only the tools that fit each message, and deterministic "arms" do the real work, so a free model only has to choose, never calculate. She listens for a wake word I trained myself, opens and closes apps, handles files, volume and screenshots, turns screenshots into todos and notes, and asks for a yes before anything risky. Every change starts from a snapshot, so it can be rolled back in one command. On my phone she runs as a sign-in web app. She also talks to Caelia, so alerts land on my desk when I am at the PC and on my phone when I am not.

Telegram · Family assistant · Free models

ProblemMy family needed one place for reminders, todos, expenses and quick answers, in the languages we speak at home.
ResultA Telegram assistant with 18 skills: research with sources, reminders, a shared family todo board and expense log, receipt and voice-note reading, translation, PDF summaries and a 7 AM briefing.

Caelia lives on Telegram and only answers family and friends I invite, each with a private notebook. She runs on a small server with her own agent runtime on Node 22 and a failover chain of free models, picked after benchmarking 13 of them. Voice notes are transcribed with Whisper, and she translates between English, Tagalog, Cebuano and Aklanon. A separate watchdog bot sends the alerts and reminders, a Cloudflare Worker checks the server is alive, and she links to A.L.I.S.O.N. on my PC. 137 unit tests cover the bridge and the skills.
