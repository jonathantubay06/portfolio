The Challenge

MOEV Solutions — a Filipino startup supplying, installing, and maintaining EV charging stations nationwide — needed a full web presence. They had no in-house development team and a tight timeline: they needed to be live within weeks, not months.

The site had to serve multiple audiences: fleet operators evaluating commercial charging solutions, residential customers exploring home installation, and partners looking for maintenance contracts. Each needed a clear, distinct path through the site.

The Approach

I took full ownership — design, development, content structure, and deployment:

  • Discovery-first: Ran a structured intake session to map their service lines (Supply, Installation, Maintenance, Consultation) to page architecture before writing a single line of code.
  • Custom WordPress theme: Built from scratch — no page builder bloat. Clean, semantic markup optimized for speed and SEO. Every page structured around a specific service vertical with clear CTAs.
  • Mobile-first responsive: The EV industry skews mobile — fleet managers and homeowners both browse on phones. Every page tested at 400px and up.
  • One-week staging preview: Deployed to a staging environment by week 2 so the client could review, provide feedback, and iterate before launch. No surprises on go-live day.
  • Hosting + email setup: Configured their domain, professional email, and hosting as part of the package — they didn't need to coordinate with multiple vendors.

The Result

Launched in 3 weeks — solo. The client received a fast, professional web presence that clearly communicates their four service lines, builds trust with enterprise and residential customers alike, and requires minimal ongoing maintenance.

Since launch, MOEV has received positive feedback from clients and partners on the site's clarity and professionalism. The site continues to serve as their primary digital storefront.
