Owner: Jojo (personal). Written 2026-10-29. Read with DESIGN.md before any UI work.

## What it is

Jonathan "Jojo" Tubay's personal portfolio. He is a full-stack engineer who builds web apps, automations and AI tools for small businesses. The site is one long page, plus case-study pages and a thanks page.

## Who visits and what success looks like

- **Hiring managers, recruiters and small-business owners** decide whether to contact him. Success means they use the contact form, email him or book a call.
- **Peers and curious visitors** come for the 3D desk and room. Success means they stay, explore, and remember the name.

Mode: Experience. The work and the 3D world lead, and the interface stays out of the way. The contact path must always be one tap away: the nav, the phone sticky "Let's talk" bar, and the footer.

## Hard constraints

- Static site with no framework and no npm install. `node build.js` turns `src/` into `dist/`, and Netlify deploys `main`.
- Netlify build credits are limited. Batch pushes, and only push changes that alter what `dist/` serves.
- Production changes need Jojo's explicit go for each change.
- The CSP is generated by build.js, which hashes the inline scripts. No inline `on...=` handlers.
- Phones must never pay for desktop effects. Desktop-only JS lives in `effects-dk.js` and loads only on `html.dk`.
- Readability: all text meets WCAG AA (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large) on both phone and desktop, in dark and light mode.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` everywhere.
